## Why ResiliChain wins the comparison

The system has an objective external environment and every decision can be checked. It can prove that an action changed inventory and route state, demonstrate a second disruption, quantify trade-offs, and independently verify service, cost, delivery, capacity, and carbon constraints. This maps directly onto the largest rubric weights rather than relying on the number of agents or model prompts.
